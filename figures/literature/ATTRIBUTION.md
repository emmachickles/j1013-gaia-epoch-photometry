# Evans et al. Andromeda panel

`evans2023_gaps_fig1.png` is the original figure image retrieved from the paper’s arXiv version, copied without alteration. It reproduces Figure 1 of Evans et al. (2023), *Gaia Data Release 3: The Gaia Andromeda Photometric Survey*, A&A 674, A4.

Source paper: https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202244204

The figure shows the source distribution in the GAPS field, with a logarithmic density scale; it is not a direct image of M31. Image source: https://arxiv.org/html/2206.05591v1/figs/GAPSAll.png. Its colours have their own scale and are not calibrated by the neighbouring all-sky panel’s colour bar.
